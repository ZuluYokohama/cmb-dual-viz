Intent

Why this exists

Attention has an interior and an exterior. Formal structure and felt pattern run on the same substrate, but most tools force you to pick one language. CMB Dual-Thread keeps both — then projects their meeting onto surfaces you can look at.

The public face is deliberately representational: art, metaphor, and plain speech an outsider can hold. The operational workshop — controls, ledger, fail-closed tags — stays inside the live engine. This page is the forest, not the trees.

How to read without lying to yourself

High score ≠ OPEN

Bright readouts, toy z-scores, and match-pull are instruments of intuition. They do not authorize discovery, funding, or safety decisions.

Fail-closed

Claims stay tagged. Promotion is human. Ingested streams stay untrusted until labeled. Nothing auto-promotes to PHYSICS-BACKED or OPEN.

Labels travel

Physics-backed, example, metaphor, derived, research — the strictest input sets the ceiling. Fake citations and invented “measured” numbers are forbidden.
